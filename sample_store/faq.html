Frequently asked questions

Do you offer gift wrapping? Yes, gift wrapping is free. Add a note at checkout and we will wrap the mug and include a blank gift card.

Do you accept custom orders? We do not accept custom orders at this time. Our small studio only produces the designs listed on this site.

How do I care for the mug? The mug is dishwasher safe, but hand washing with a soft sponge keeps the glaze looking best for longer.

How can I contact you? Email us at support@momoceramics.example and we reply within one business day.
