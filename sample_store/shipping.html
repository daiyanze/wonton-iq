Shipping and delivery

Orders are processed in 2 to 3 business days. We ship to Japan, the United States, Canada and the European Union.

Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days within Japan and 5 to 10 business days for the United States, Canada and the European Union.

Shipping is free for orders over 80 US dollars; otherwise a flat 6 dollar shipping fee applies. A tracking number is emailed to you when your order ships.
