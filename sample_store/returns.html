Returns and refunds policy

You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

If your mug arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

For regular returns the customer pays return shipping. For damaged items we cover all costs. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item.
